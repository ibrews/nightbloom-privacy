# Nightbloom Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 24, 2026*

Nightbloom is an augmented-reality fireworks app by Alex Coulombe Presents. This policy explains what the
app does with information on your device.

## What Nightbloom collects

**Nothing.** Nightbloom has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it
does not send any data from your device to us or to anyone else.

## Camera, depth and motion

Nightbloom uses the camera, depth sensors (LiDAR or TrueDepth, where available) and motion sensors to place
fireworks in the real world and hide them behind real objects. This processing happens entirely on your
device, in real time. Camera frames and depth data are never stored, recorded (unless you start a recording),
or transmitted.

## Photos and videos

When you tap the photo or record button, Nightbloom saves the picture or video to your Photos library. It asks
only for permission to **add** to your library; it cannot see or read your existing photos. Saved media stay
on your device under your control.

## Settings

Your Nightbloom settings (which fireworks are enabled, sound, occlusion mode and so on) are stored only on your
device.

## TestFlight and Apple

If you use a beta build through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, usage statistics and any feedback you
choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. That information is handled by Apple
and used only to fix and improve the app.

## Children

Nightbloom does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
